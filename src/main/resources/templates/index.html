About Northwind Database

Northwind is a fictional company frequently used as a sample database for learning database management, SQL, and application development.

It represents an international trading company that imports and exports specialty food products around the world. The database contains realistic business information and relationships that help developers and students understand how enterprise systems work.

The Northwind database includes interconnected entities such as:

👥
Customers

Store customer information and relationships.

📦
Products

Manage inventory and product details.

📝
Orders

Track sales and customer purchases.

🚚
Suppliers

Manage suppliers and logistics operations.

📊
Dashboard Reports

Analyze sales, customers, and inventory through an interactive dashboard.

⚡
CRUD Operations

Easily create, update, delete, and manage business data.

🔐
Secure Access

Role-based authentication and authorization for users.
